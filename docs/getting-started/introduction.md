
# Introduction to ElvioNode

## What is ElvioNode?

ElvioNode is a modern, self-hosted game server control panel designed to simplify the deployment, management, monitoring, and automation of game servers.

Whether you operate a small gaming community, a large multiplayer network, or a commercial game server hosting business, ElvioNode provides a centralized platform for managing your infrastructure.

## Our Philosophy

ElvioNode is built around five fundamental principles:

- **Performance** — A fast and responsive management experience.
- **Simplicity** — Powerful functionality without unnecessary complexity.
- **Control** — Maintain ownership of your infrastructure and data.
- **Automation** — Reduce repetitive administrative tasks.
- **Scalability** — Grow your infrastructure as your requirements evolve.

## Self-Hosted by Design

ElvioNode runs on your own infrastructure rather than requiring you to host your game servers through a third-party hosting service.

You retain control over:

- Your hardware and computing resources.
- Your game server files and configurations.
- Your network infrastructure.
- Your users and access permissions.
- Your server deployment environment.

ElvioNode provides the management layer while you remain in control of the underlying infrastructure.

## Platform Architecture

ElvioNode is organized around three fundamental components.

### Control Panel

The web-based interface used to manage servers, monitor performance, configure settings, manage users, and administer connected infrastructure.

### Nodes

A node represents a machine connected to your ElvioNode environment.

Depending on your license, multiple nodes can be managed through a single centralized control panel.

### Game Servers

Game servers are individual server instances managed through ElvioNode.

A node can accommodate multiple game servers, subject to available hardware resources and applicable license limitations.

## Multi-Game Support

ElvioNode is designed as a multi-game management platform.

Game-specific functionality, installation procedures, and configuration options may vary depending on the game and server software being used.

Minecraft-specific functionality is part of the broader ElvioNode ecosystem rather than the sole purpose of the platform.

## Licensing

ElvioNode offers two licensing models:

### Perpetual Licenses

Designed for individuals, communities, and server networks that prefer a one-time payment.

### Subscription Licenses

Designed for commercial hosting providers requiring scalable customer management, billing integration, and white-label functionality.

For current pricing and license limitations, visit the [official website](https://elvionode.com).
